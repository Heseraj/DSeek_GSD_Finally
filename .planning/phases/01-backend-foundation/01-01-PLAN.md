## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-01 | Tampering | SQL construction in app/db/database.py and portfolio/watchlist services | medium | mitigate | All queries use parameterized sqlite3 placeholders; f-string SQL is prohibited; schema uses UNIQUE(user_id,ticker) to prevent duplicate rows |
| T-01-02 | Information Disclosure | GET /api/portfolio and GET /api/watchlist responses | low | accept | Single-user simulated data; no secrets or credentials returned |
| T-01-03 | Denial of Service | GET /api/stream/prices long-lived SSE connections | low | accept | Version-based emit produces no unbounded queue; EventSource reconnect is client-driven |
| T-01-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | low | accept | No new dependencies introduced in this plan; stdlib sqlite3 plus existing fastapi/uvicorn only |
